Originally, I wanted to go with a design inpired by baroque motifs from baroque architecture as I play a lot of baroque music. After trying to find a way of drawing those motifs and making them easy to manipulate to commmunicate the time, I found that doing this would be too hard to finish between last Monday (10 March) and the due date. I instead thought of a new idea that wouldn't shift away from doing something music inspired. The new idea is a practice room with various items that musicians use. First is a metronome which the arm swinging back and forth could track seconds. Next is a tuner or music stand to track minutes. The tuner will display the minute and then show that it is getting closer and closer to being in tune until the next minute starts. The music stand could turn pages showing a number with the current minute on it. Lastly, to represent hours, I would have a window that shows the skys changing colors. I thought it would be good since I would often lose track of time when practicing and only notice I had been practicing for several hours when the sun started setting or if it was dark outside.
