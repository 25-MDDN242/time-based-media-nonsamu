## MDDN 242 Project 1: Time-based Media  

## Clock Name: The Practice Room
### Name: Samuel Non

Intentions:
Originally, I wanted to go with a design inpired by baroque motifs from baroque architecture as I play a lot of baroque music. After trying to find a way of drawing those motifs and making them easy to manipulate to commmunicate the time, I found that doing this would be too hard to finish between last Monday (10 March) and the due date. I instead thought of a new idea that wouldn't shift away from doing something music inspired. The new idea is a practice room with various items that musicians use. First is a metronome which the arm swinging back and forth could track seconds. Next is a tuner or music stand to track minutes. The tuner will display the minute and then show that it is getting closer and closer to being in tune until the next minute starts. The music stand could turn pages showing a number with the current minute on it. Lastly, to represent hours, I would have a window that shows the skys changing colors. I thought it would be good since I would often lose track of time when practicing and only notice I had been practicing for several hours when the sun started setting or if it was dark outside.

I kept the metronome and tuner. The tuner uses random numbers to show the 'note' or time being 'out of tune' but the amount of bars that can be active becomes smaller the closer the new minute gets in order to simulate tuning an instrument. The metronome is still tracking seconds and is set to 60bpm as such. I had a very boring design for a window that I changed using different dimensions and colors to make it more interesting. I also added stars at night time that fade out during day. Lastly, to fill in empty space I added a couple of Marshall amplifiers to make the room seem more like a practice room. I wanted to try add a VoxAC15 but I was having trouble with getting the grill right with the limited time I had left. 

For the alarm functionality, I made the arm of the metronome stop entirely and the tuner show a fully red screen. The amplifier speakers start pulsing at 217 beats per minute as I tried to add a music file for a cover I made of a song. It would have matched the tempo of the music that would have played when the alarm goes off.
